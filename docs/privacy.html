Privacy Policy

Deutsch with Ayan (com.deutschwithayan.app) — Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Summary: Deutsch with Ayan is built with learner privacy as its top priority. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data, sensitive identifiers, or usage logs. All learning progress is stored solely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Deutsch with Ayan does not require user registration, account creation, or login. As a result, we do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, address).
  • Financial, billing, or payment information.
  • Precise or approximate geographical location data.
  • Contacts, photos, media, or files from your device.
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs.

2. Local Data Storage on Your Device

To provide an uninterrupted learning experience and offline functionality, the app saves educational progress locally on your device using secure local device storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Lesson completion status (A1, A2, B1, B2).
  • Daily learning streaks and practice points.
  • Vocabulary mastery and game scores (Article Battle, Memory Pairs, Sentence Builder).
  • Language preferences (German, Arabic, English audio/text toggles).

This data never leaves your device and is erased automatically if you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in your device settings.

3. Speech Synthesis and Audio

The app utilizes standard operating system speech synthesis (such as Android Speech Services) to pronounce German, Arabic, and English words. Audio synthesis operates locally or via system TTS services. The app does not record or transmit microphone audio.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no tracking scripts, and no marketing beacons. We do not sell or trade any user information to any third parties.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app provides language learning content suitable for all ages, including children. Because we do not collect, store, or share any personal information from any user, our service fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

6. Security

Because no personal data is transmitted over the internet to remote servers, your information is protected by the inherent security of your operating system's local application sandbox.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
